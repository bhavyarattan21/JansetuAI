Why Grievances Fail in India

Over 70% of citizens give up on civic complaints due to institutional friction.

🏛️
Jurisdictional Ping-Pong
Is a pothole under PWD, Municipal Corporation, or NHAI? Over 40% of grievances are bounced or rejected without resolution due to wrong department routing.
📜
Informal, Weak Drafting
Citizens lack access to formal legal terminology. Without citing statutory Citizen Charters or Municipal Acts, complaints are treated as low-priority informal notes.
⏳
Zero SLA Accountability
Portals assign 7-day or 15-day SLAs, but when deadlines pass, citizens have no automated escalation or Right to Information (RTI) mechanism to hold nodal officers accountable.

🔒 ENTERPRISE SECURITY

PostgreSQL Schema & Zero-Trust RLS

Relational PostgreSQL Tables
• profiles: ID, Full Name, Email, Language, Ward Profile
• complaints: Category, Dept, Portal Ref, Status, SLA Expected By
• complaint_photos: Storage Path, Kind (Before/After), Timestamp
• complaint_events: Immutable Audit Trail of Status Transitions
• chat_messages: AI Copilot Historical Sessions
Zero-Trust Privacy Policies
Row Level Security (RLS): Citizens can only query and mutate records where auth.uid() = user_id.

Private Storage Policies: Images stored at {user_id}/{complaint_id}/{uuid}.jpg. No public read access; images require time-limited signed URLs.

Citizen Privacy Notice: Guidelines prompt citizens to avoid capturing faces or vehicle plates.
🎙️ Presenter Script: "We prioritized citizen privacy from Day 1. Evidence photos are stored in private buckets and are never accessible via static links. Every table is protected by PostgreSQL Row Level Security."
